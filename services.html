What moves a quote

Instead of a price list — which would be wrong in one direction or the other before the scope is known — the five things that decide it.

  1. How many pages, and how many of them are genuinely different
  2. How many languages, and whether they share one data set
  3. What you edit yourself after delivery
  4. Which systems it has to talk to
  5. What happens after launch: hosting, backups, corrections
